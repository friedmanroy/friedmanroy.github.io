I am currently a postdoc at [Nikolaus Rajewsky's lab](https://rajewsky-lab.github.io/) in [BIMSB](https://www.mdc-berlin.de/themen/mdc-bimsb). 

I finished my PhD at the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://www.cs.huji.ac.il/) under the joint supervision of [Yair Weiss](https://www.cs.huji.ac.il/~yweiss/) and [Mor Nitzan](https://www.nitzanlab.com/). My PhD interests spanned computational imaging, generative models for images, and dynamical processes in single-cell biology.

During my PhD, I was also the TA for HUJI's course on Bayesian Machine Learning, which Yair Weiss and I have built together. I'm in the process of uploading the course material to the ["Bayesian Machine Learning"](https://friedmanroy.github.io/BML/) section of this site.
