Roy Friedman

I am currently a postdoc at Nikolaus Rajewsky’s lab in BIMSB.

I finished my PhD at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem under the joint supervision of Yair Weiss and Mor Nitzan. My PhD interests spanned computational imaging, generative models for images, and dynamical processes in single-cell biology.

During my PhD, I was also the TA for HUJI’s course on Bayesian Machine Learning, which Yair Weiss and I have built together. I’m in the process of uploading the course material to the “Bayesian Machine Learning” section of this site.
